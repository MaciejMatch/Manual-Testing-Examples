### TC-01 – Successful Registration
Preconditions: User does not have an account
Steps:
1. Open Demoblaze
2. Click "Sign up"
3. Enter valid credentials
4. Submit
Expected Result: Account is created successfully

### TC-02 – Successful Login
Preconditions: Account exists
Steps:
1. Open login
2. Enter valid username and password
3. Submit
Expected Result: User is logged in

### TC-03 – Browse and Select Products
Preconditions: User logged in
Steps:
1. Navigate to product category
2. Select a product
Expected Result: Product page opens correctly

### TC-04 – Add Products to Cart
Preconditions: User logged in
Steps:
1. Click "Add to cart" on product page
Expected Result: Product is added to cart

### TC-05 – View Cart
Preconditions: Product in cart
Steps:
1. Open shopping cart
Expected Result: Correct product details, quantity, and price displayed

### TC-06 – Checkout
Preconditions: Product in cart, user logged in
Steps:
1. Click "Place Order"
2. Fill in order details
3. Confirm purchase
Expected Result: Order confirmation is displayed

### TC-07 – Invalid Login
Preconditions: Account exists
Steps:
1. Open login
2. Enter invalid username or password
3. Submit
Expected Result: Proper error message is displayed
